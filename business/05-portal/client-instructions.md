# Your client portal

*This page is written for the client. It is the plain-language source for what
goes in the welcome pack and for anything sent when someone asks how the portal
works. Send it as it is, or lift from it. Everything in it is true of the portal
as it runs today.*

---

## What the portal is

It is your own page on our website, showing everything we are doing for you:

- **Quotes** — what we have quoted, for how much, whether it is still valid, and
  the PDF to download.
- **Projects** — what we are building, how far along it is, and what the next
  milestone is.
- **Meetings** — what is booked, when, where, and a join link where there is one.
- **Documents** — signed agreements, reports, anything else we have sent you.

It is there so you never have to email us to ask where something is. Open it and
look.

## How to sign in

1. Go to **nabl.agency/portal**
2. Type in your access key
3. Press **Access portal**

Your key looks like this:

```
NABL-7K3M-QP24-XW9B
```

It is four blocks separated by hyphens. The first block is a short version of
your business name, so you can tell at a glance that it is yours.

A few things that make it easier:

- **Capitals do not matter.** The box converts what you type.
- **The hyphens do matter.** Include them.
- **There is no letter O and no letter I in a key**, and no lower-case L. If
  something looks like one of those, it is a zero, a one, or the digit next to
  it. We chose the characters that way on purpose so a key can be read out over
  the phone without confusion.
- **There is no password.** The key is the whole thing. You do not need to
  create an account, confirm an email address or set anything up.

### You will be signed out when you close the tab

This is deliberate rather than a fault. Nothing about your sign-in is stored on
your computer, so there is nothing left behind on a shared or borrowed machine.
When you come back, enter the key again.

## Looking after your key

**Treat it exactly like a password.** It is the only thing protecting your
quotes, your project details and your documents. Anyone who has it can see all
of them.

Please do:

- Save it somewhere only your business can get to — a password manager is ideal,
  a note in your own files is fine.
- Share it only with people in your business who should see your account.
- Tell us straight away if you think it has gone somewhere it should not.

Please do not:

- Post it in a group chat, a shared document or a public channel.
- Forward it on to a supplier, a contractor or anyone outside your business.
- Leave it on screen in a meeting that is being recorded or shared.

## If something goes wrong

**"It says my key is invalid."**
Check the hyphens are there and nothing has been cut off when it was copied. If
it still will not go in, email us and we will check the key on your record.

**"It says there have been too many attempts."**
That is the system slowing down repeated wrong guesses. Wait fifteen minutes and
try again. If it happens when you are certain the key is right, tell us — it
means something is trying keys against your account, and we would want to know.

**"I have lost my key."**
Email us. We will create a new one and send it over. Your old one stops working
the moment we do, and nothing in your portal is lost.

**"I think someone else has seen my key."**
Email us and say so, even if you are not sure. We will replace it within
minutes, and it costs you nothing. There is no version of this where telling us
was the wrong call.

**"Someone has left my company and they had the key."**
Tell us and we will issue a new one. At the moment there is one key per
business rather than one per person, so the way to remove one person's access is
to change the key for everyone. Let us know and we will handle it.

**"Nothing is showing in one of the sections."**
An empty section means there is nothing in it yet, not that something has
broken. If you are expecting something specific and it is not there, tell us.

## What the portal will not do

Worth knowing up front, so you are not looking for a button that does not exist.

- **It is read-only.** You can look at everything and download anything, but you
  cannot accept a quote, upload a file or change a project in it. Reply to us in
  the usual way and we will update it.
- **It will not email you when something changes.** There are no notifications.
  We will tell you when there is something to look at.
- **Documents open through a temporary link.** When you click to open a quote or
  a document, we create a private link that works for about an hour and then
  expires. That is what keeps your files private. If you copy that link and try
  it the next day it will not work — open the document from the portal again
  instead.
