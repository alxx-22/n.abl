tested and committed but has not run against the live project. Until it does,

## What to expect

The migration is `if not exists` / `or replace` throughout and was checked for
idempotency locally, so a second run is harmless. It adds three columns to
`marketing_sends`, all nullable, so it cannot fail on existing rows — there are
none, because nothing has been sent.

The one thing that could genuinely differ on live is the grants, for the reason
in step 2. That is why it is called out rather than left to a general "check it
worked".
