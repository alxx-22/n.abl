# AI, where it earns its place

A short launch film for the n.abl AI service, 58 seconds, cut for every
social format from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.

Everything in it (the voice, the music, the sound effects and every frame) is
generated by the scripts in this folder. No stock footage, stock music or
licensed voice is involved. See [`README.md`](README.md) to rebuild it.

---

## 1. The idea

Most AI launch videos promise everything. This one shows three specific jobs
(reading, sorting and drafting) and then does the thing nobody else in the
category does on camera: it says when you do *not* need AI.

That is the brand position (`business/01-positioning/messaging-spine.md` §8)
turned into a story beat, and it is the moment people will remember. The
campaign line comes from the site's own AI capability chip:

> **AI, where it earns its place.**

The service it describes is `business/knowledge/services/ai/service.md`:
reading, sorting or drafting done by someone whose time is worth more, with a
person checking anything that leaves the building.

## 2. Brand checks

- Warm espresso ground, cream light, one amber accent used sparingly. Glows are
  a bloom, never neon.
- Space Grotesk for display, Inter Tight for interface, JetBrains Mono for
  figures and code. The self-hosted files from `public/fonts/`.
- The wordmark is drawn, so the end card *draws* it: the stroke paths from
  `public/brand/wordmark.svg`, written on with their real geometry, and the
  square dot landing last in amber.
- The spark is the house AI mark (`src/components/scenes/ai.js`). It appears
  only where it labels the AI, and pulses only while the AI is working.
- No invented clients, statistics, testimonials or percentages. The documents
  and emails in the film are illustrative props.
- Never "AI-powered", "cutting-edge", "transform" or "agency". British English.
  The limit is stated twice: an unsure answer goes to a person, and a job that
  does not need AI does not get it.
- The name is voiced "enable".

## 4. Animation script

Scene timings are set by the voice and snapped to the music's bar grid at
build time, so each cut lands on a beat. The words on screen carry the story
for anyone watching muted.

**01 · Read, retype (line 1).** Near-black. A caret blinks. "Someone reads a
document." fades in word by word behind a reading highlight. The second line
is *typed* on screen character by character, with key clicks, and then echoes
down the frame in fading copies: the same work, again and again.

**02 · The pile (line 2).** Cream paper documents drop in on each word, an
invoice, a referral letter, an application form, each with its own layout and
a mono tag. More follow until the frame is a scattered pile in perspective. A
week strip runs across the bottom, Monday to Friday, the same block filling
each day.

**03 · The turn (line 3).** The pile freezes and is pulled into the centre,
collapsing into the amber spark with a soft bloom. The music opens up. Title:
**AI, where it earns its place** with the square dot.

**04 · It reads (lines 4 and 5).** An invoice on the left, an empty record on
the right. A scan line passes down the page; fields are boxed and fly across
into the record, typing themselves in. One field comes up amber, *Not sure*,
and is routed to a person. A cursor comes in and confirms it. The cursor is
only ever a person.

**05 · It sorts (line 6).** An inbox of real-sounding messages. The spark reads
each one, tags it by meaning, and the rows fly into four desks: Bookings,
Accounts, Sales, Customer care.

**06 · It drafts (line 7).** A customer question. The spark writes a reply word
by word from the business's own records, and a source chip appears beneath it.
A person reviews and sends. Caption: *A person checks anything that leaves the
building.*

**07 · The boring version (lines 8 and 9).** The drums drop out. "Doesn't need
AI?" The spark switches off. A code card types four lines of plain Python, a
rule and no model. "Cheaper." "Breaks less often." land on the beat as the
groove comes back.

**08 · The terms (line 10).** Three statements snap in on the beat, numbered in
mono: No retainer. A price before we start. You own what we build.

**09 · End card (line 11).** The wordmark draws itself stroke by stroke in
cream; the amber square dot drops onto the baseline with a low hit and a bloom.
Beneath it: *AI, where it earns its place.* and `nabl.agency`.

## 5. Sound

- **Music.** Synthesised for this film at 110 BPM: a filtered, ticking intro
  under the problem; a riser into the turn; warm pads, a plucked arpeggio, sub
  bass and a soft four-on-the-floor through the three jobs; a breakdown for the
  honest beat; a final lift into the logo hit and a long tail.
- **Effects.** Typing, paper, whooshes on transitions, a scan sweep, UI clicks
  and ticks, a shimmer on the spark, and a sub hit on the dot.
- **Mix.** Music ducks under the voice. Master at about −14 LUFS integrated,
  true peak below −1 dBTP, which suits every social platform.
