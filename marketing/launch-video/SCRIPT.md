# Put AI to work

A 33-second launch film for the n.abl AI service, cut for every social format
from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.

Everything in it (the voice, the music, the sound effects and every frame) is
generated by the scripts in this folder. No stock footage, stock music or
licensed voice is involved. See [`README.md`](README.md) to rebuild it.

---

## 1. The idea

AI is how business runs now, and n.abl builds it into yours. The film opens on
that idea as a system update for the whole world, lands on one word, **AI**,
and then moves fast: three customer-facing agents in quick succession, then a
wall of everything else AI can take on, then what the business gets out of it.

- **Pace.** Ten shots in 33 seconds, every cut on the beat of a 124 BPM track.
  No shot holds longer than the line that goes with it.
- **Breadth over depth.** The agents get a few seconds each: booking,
  answering questions around the clock, handling complaints and queries. The
  wall shows 24 jobs at once and names four of them.
- **Energy.** A livelier British voice, a driving track with a drop on "AI!",
  and motion in every frame: a turning globe, a flight through the business,
  whip pans, square wipes, a camera sweep across the wall, words that slam in.

The line it ends on: **Put AI to work.**

## 2. Brand notes

- Warm espresso ground, cream light, amber as the accent. The square dot does
  the heavy lifting: the square wipes are the dot filling the frame, and the
  last shot gathers everything into the dot before the wordmark draws itself
  around it.
- Space Grotesk for display, Inter Tight for interface, JetBrains Mono for
  labels and figures. The self-hosted files from `public/fonts/`.
- The wordmark is drawn, so the end card draws it: the stroke paths from
  `public/brand/wordmark.svg` written on with their real geometry.
- The spark is the house AI mark (`src/components/scenes/ai.js`).
- No invented clients, statistics or testimonials. Names, orders and messages
  in the interfaces are illustrative props.
- The name is voiced "enable".
- This cut deliberately leads with AI and runs at a higher energy than the
  plain, quiet register in `business/02-brand/voice-and-tone.md`. That was the
  brief for this film.

## 3. Voiceover

Voice: Kokoro `bm_fable` (British English, Apache-2.0 model) at 1.2× speed.
Chosen because it has about twice the pitch movement of the other British
voices, which is what makes a read sound lively rather than level.

## 4. Animation script

Each shot starts on a beat; the words on screen carry the story for anyone
watching muted.

**01 · Boot.** A globe of dots grows out of a single point and turns. A chip
reads *Installing AI* and counts to 100%. "The world just got a new operating
system" writes itself beside it, with an amber underline under *operating
system*.

**02 · AI.** The globe falls into the centre. On "AI!" the word slams in at
full frame height with the spark beside it: rays, flying amber squares, three
shockwave rings and a bloom, on the drop. The camera flies through the letters.

**03 · Into.** A flight through the business: sixteen system tiles (calendar,
inbox, live chat, invoices, reviews, CRM, phone calls and more) rush past in
depth and light up as they are connected. The line sits in the middle with the
drawn wordmark in place of the name. An amber square wipe cuts to the agents.

**04 · Books appointments.** A booking chat on a website: *Can I book in for
Friday afternoon?* The agent offers 2pm, the customer says yes, and the Friday
2pm slot in the diary fills amber with a burst of squares. Whip pan.

**05 · Answers questions 24/7.** Three questions answered in quick succession
while a dial runs from afternoon to night, sun setting, moon rising, the clock
rolling from 14:00 to 02:00. Whip pan.

**06 · Handles complaints and queries.** A damaged-order complaint arrives. It
is tagged *Complaint · Urgent · Order #4471 · Replacement*, a reply writes
itself, and an escalation gauge swings from red to green. *Resolved.*

**07 · The wall.** The camera starts close on one tile and pulls back to
reveal a tilted wall of 24 AI jobs, lighting up in a wave as the camera sweeps
across it. As each one is said, its tile lifts out of the wall and the caption
changes: reads your paperwork, sorts your inbox, drafts your replies, chases
every lead. The camera flies through into the next shot.

**08 · Gains.** Three words slam in on the beat, each with its own motion: a
stack of paper that empties for *Less admin*, speed lines and a stopwatch for
*Faster answers*, five stars popping for *Happier customers*. Square wipe.

**09 · Yours.** Blocks fly in from every side and assemble while "Built for
your business. Yours to keep." lands, with *No retainers · you own everything
we build* under it. Then every block collapses into a single amber square.

**10 · End card.** That square is the dot. It glides to its place while the
wordmark draws itself around it, and lands on "n.abl" with a hit and a ring.
*Put AI to work*, then *Book a free discovery call · nabl.agency*.

## 5. Sound

- **Music.** Synthesised for this film at 124 BPM in D major: a ticking,
  filtered pulse under the opening; the drums drop out for the build; the drop
  lands on "AI!"; four-on-the-floor with offbeat chord stabs, plucked
  sixteenths and sub bass through the agents, the wall and the gains; a short
  breakdown under "Yours to keep"; a snare roll into the logo and a resolve on
  D.
- **Effects.** A boot chime, a riser and impact on the drop, message sent and
  received sounds, whip pans, square wipes, tile flips, ticks, stars, the
  drawing of the wordmark and the hit when the dot lands.
- **Mix.** Music ducks under the voice from the script's own timings. Master
  at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak below −1 dBTP on the delivered AAC.
