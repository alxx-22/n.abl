# Paperwork, read and filed: the Document AI reel

A 16-second reel for n.abl's Document AI, cut to a supplied track with no
voice, in every social format from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.
Everything it claims is the site's own line for the service: it reads
invoices, forms and delivery notes, pulls out what matters and files it,
with a person checking anything it is unsure of.

## 1. The brief

Two supplied clips. The first gives the track: the first 16 seconds of its
audio, used as they are (`music/track.wav`). The second gives the look: a
16-second product film for a contract tool, light and editorial, which this
reel follows in style, not in content.

What the look is, frame by frame: warm off-white paper; ink type, a serif
for headlines and a small clean sans for the interface; one brand accent
with a soft highlight of it; soft grey cards with round corners; status
tags in red, amber and green; tracked changes in red and blue. The product's
own interface is recreated and driven by a cursor: a kinetic headline whose
highlighted word rolls like a slot machine, a document dragged into a drop
zone, the drop zone becoming a search bar, a table filling in row by row
with *Processing…* loaders, a card clicked open into a chart, a big counter
among floating file icons, a prompt typed out, *Analyzing*, a tree of
related documents, a clause highlighted and redlined, *Changes Implemented*,
and the logo morphing on the beat over painted scenes before a clean
lockup. A beat and a half per idea, shapes carried from one moment into the
next, smooth camera pushes and blur.

Here, the same grammar in n.abl's own palette and type: warm paper and ink,
the amber of the full stop as the one accent (its soft tint for highlights),
Space Grotesk for headlines, Inter Tight for the interface, JetBrains Mono
for figures.

## 3. Sound

**Music**: the supplied track, played as it is from its start
(`MUSIC = "track"`), easing out over the last half second.

**Effects**, clean and under the track: a pop as the line appears, a tick
and a swish as each word rolls, a drop for the full stop; a stream of air
and paper as the stack comes in, a pop and a drop as it lands, a chime for
*3 documents*, a swish as the zone folds; a scan as the line reads the
invoice, a blip as each value lands, a chime for the match, a tick for the
flag, a click and a drop as it is approved; a rush of air as the camera
slams back, a tick for each row read and a pop for each one filed; typing
as the count rolls and a chime as it settles; a whoosh and a drop as each
stroke of the mark is drawn, a pop and a double drop for the full stop; the
name drawing and a shimmer under it.

**Mix**: the music about 15 dB over the effects; −13.4 LUFS integrated,
true peak below −1 dBTP on the delivered AAC (the limiter's ceiling is
−3 dB for this film, as the track is mastered hot).

## 4. Delivery

Rendered at twice the size: each format at its usual size (scaled down from
the large frames) and as `-4k`; H.264 High, 60 fps, AAC 256k.
