# Paperwork, read and filed: the Document AI reel

A 16-second reel for n.abl's Document AI, cut to a supplied track with no
voice, in every social format from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.
What it claims is the site's own line for the service: it reads invoices,
forms and delivery notes, pulls out what matters and files it.

## 1. The brief

- **Music:** the first 16 seconds of a supplied clip's audio, used as they
  are (`music/track.wav`).
- **Story:** up to the build, the problem, and not as volume or hours lost
  but as tedium: how many fields one document needs before finance, legal
  and ops have what they need, each typed in by hand. Specific, a little
  dry, and unlike the usual sales line. On the build's fast hits, n.abl
  Document AI flashed in and out on each. From the drop, the answer.
- **Sync:** everything on the track's hits, slower and simpler than the first
  cut, and more time on the end screen.
- **Look:** the 2026-11 brand (`business/02-brand/brand-guidelines.md`):
  Manjari, the lit ground, and ember, amber and ice keeping their meanings.

## 2. The track

## 3. The reel

| Time | Shot | What happens, and on what |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | **Fields** | *One document.* A supplier pack lands on the first pluck. Then *47 fields to fill.*: a New supplier form, and on each pluck a group of empty fields stacks into it, finance first (supplier, VAT number, nominal code, cost centre, sort code...), then legal (contract ref, notice period, liability cap, governing law...), then ops (deliver to, batch, use by, allergens...). The form scrolls on past the frame and its counter climbs: 2, 4, 9, 14, 20, 27, 34, 47. |
| 3.28 | **Type** | The track goes silent. Field 7 of 47, VAT number, typed by hand a key at a time, G, GB, GB 2, GB 24, and the line arrives a word a key: *One. At. A. Time.* |
| 4.36 | **Loop** | The plucks come back as the routine: *Copy. Paste. Check. Repeat.*, a word a pluck. On the swell the whole line comes again, and again on every pluck after, each pushing the last up and dimmer, a wall of it, while the chip crawls from field 8 to field 13 of 47. |
| 7.06 | **Flash** | The three build hits: n.abl Document AI flashed in and out on each, larger each time, the light flaring with every hit and the fluted glass coming in, fine reeds first. |
| 7.87 | **Fix** | The drop: the same form, all 47 fields at once, and on the drop it fills itself in one wave from top to bottom, each field lit ice and ticked. A line a clap: *Every field, filled.* (8.41); *Sent to finance and legal.* (9.49) as the form goes out as three cards, Finance 20 fields, Legal 14, Ops 13; *47 fields. None typed.* (10.57) as each is ticked. The reeds breathe on every kick. |
| 12.20 | **End** | On the new phrase the aurora rises and the name draws in: the n. and its dot on the kick, *abl* on the half beat; *Document AI* (12.74), *Paperwork, read and filed.* (13.28), *nabl.agency* (13.82); the dot pulses on the claps. Held to 16.0. |

All names, numbers and references are props: Harbour Foods and every value in
the form, and the count of 47.

## 4. Look

The 2026-11 brand, applied as section 8 of the guidelines asks:

- **Type:** Manjari. Thin for the statements, Regular for labels, Bold for
  emphasis. JetBrains Mono only for the invoice's codes, as product codes.
- **Ground:** the lit ground, the reference demo's own shader
  (`ground.js`, lifted from `business/02-brand/reference/nabl-scroll-story.html`
  unchanged), its state driven by the timeline instead of the scroll, with the
  site's grain over it. It follows the brand's story: wisps and ember building
  through the problem, low in the silence and at their height in the loop; dark
  between the build hits, flaring on each as the fluted glass comes in; through
  the fix the glass, with ice rising as the work runs; aurora settling behind
  the name. The light lifts on each pluck, and after the drop the reeds and the
  aurora breathe on every kick.
- **Colour, by meaning:** ember for the problem (the fields still to fill,
  *by hand*, the field count in the loop), amber for n.abl (the square full
  stops, the logo's dot, the cards' markers), ice for the AI at work (every
  field it fills and ticks, the count of 47, the cards' ticks). Dark is most of every
  frame; words sit on a dark field, kept clear by shields in the ground.
- **The logo** is the drawn artwork, cream with the amber dot, on the dark.

## 5. Sound

**Music:** the supplied track, played as it is from its start, easing out
over the last half second.

**Effects,** under the track and on its hits: paper as the document lands,
a pop for each group of fields; four key clicks in the silence, the loudest
thing there, one for each word of *One. At. A. Time.*; a click, a pop, a tick
and a swish for *Copy. Paste. Check. Repeat.*, and a flip for each repeat;
on the drop a whoosh and a scan as the form fills, a chime when it is full;
a swish and a pop for each card as it is sent, a chime for none typed; the
name drawn and a shimmer under the line.

**Mix:** −13.4 LUFS integrated, true peak below −1 dBTP on the delivered AAC
(the limiter's ceiling is −3 dB for this film, as the track is mastered hot).

## 6. Delivery

Rendered at twice the size: each format at its usual size (scaled down from
the large frames) and as `-4k`; H.264 High, 60 fps, AAC 256k.
