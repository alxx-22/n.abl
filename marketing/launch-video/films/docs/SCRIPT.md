# Paperwork, read and filed: the Document AI reel

A 20-second reel for n.abl's Document AI, cut to a supplied track with no
voice, in every social format from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16.
What it claims is the site's own line for the service: it reads invoices,
forms and delivery notes, pulls out what matters and files it.

## 1. The brief

- **Music:** 4 to 24 seconds of a supplied clip's audio, used as it is
  (`music/track20.wav`, cut from the clip). The first cut used its first 16
  seconds (`music/track.wav`, kept).
- **Story:** a story anyone in a small business knows, told slowly enough to
  follow: a delivery arrives, its note goes to the computer and someone has
  to type it all in, then it goes onto the stack with all the others. Then
  the answer, after the supplied product film: the stack picked up and
  dropped into Document AI's upload box; one sheet filling, a row a document
  and a column a field; thousands of documents counted up; the name.
- **Sync:** consistent the whole way through, not just at the cuts: every
  hit of the track moves something. The frame breathes on each hit; the
  camera moves in steps that land on the beats; captions arrive a word a
  beat; things land on kicks and claps; rows fill and the count ticks on the
  sixteenths; the aurora pulses with it all.
- **Sound:** nothing but whooshes under the track, and the whole a little
  quieter than the other films.
- **Look:** an ad, in the 2026-11 brand (`business/02-brand/brand-guidelines.md`):
  Manjari, the brand's aurora as the only background, and ember, amber and ice
  keeping their meanings. The parcel is a cream box in three dimensions; the
  backlog is one tall stack of paper, not a heap.

## 2. The track

111 BPM. In the supplied clip its first beat is 0.304 s in; the film starts
4 s in, so the clip's beat *k* falls at 0.304 − 4 + *k* × 0.5405 s. In the
film:

| Time | Clip beat | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 0.39 to 2.82 s | 7.5 to 12 | soft plucks on the half beats; a hi-hat swell at 1.74 |
| 3.06, 3.33, 3.60 s | 12.5, 13, 13.5 | three loud hits, kick and hat together: the build |
| 3.87 s | 14 | the drop: a kick on every beat from here, a clap on every other |
| 8.20, 12.52, 16.84 s | 22, 30, 38 | new phrases |
| 19.0 s | 42 | the kick drops out for the last second |

## 4. Look

The 2026-11 brand, applied as section 8 of the guidelines asks:

- **Type:** Manjari. Thin for the statements, Regular for labels, Bold for
  emphasis. JetBrains Mono only for the sheet's codes, as product codes.
- **Ground:** the brand's aurora and nothing else, from the reference demo's
  own shader (`ground.js`, lifted from
  `business/02-brand/reference/nabl-scroll-story.html` unchanged), its state
  driven by the timeline, with the site's grain over it. Warm while the problem
  is told, cooling to ice as the answer runs, settling behind the name; its
  curtains swing and brighten on every hit.
- **Colour, by meaning:** ember for the problem (what is left to type in, the
  field being typed by hand), amber for n.abl (the square full stops, the
  logo's dot, the panels' markers), ice for the AI at work (the upload box
  reading, every value it fills, *Filed*, the live chip on the count). Dark is
  most of every frame; words sit on a dark field, kept clear by shields in the
  ground and a dark field of their own where they pass over paper. The box and
  the paper are the only other things in it: a cream box, and a stack of cream
  paper.
- **The logo** is the drawn artwork, cream with the amber dot, on the dark.

## 5. Sound

**Music:** the supplied track, 4 to 24 seconds of it, played as it is,
easing out over the last 0.6 seconds.

**Effects:** whooshes only, under the track: the box coming down, the note
going to the computer, the note onto the stack and each step it grows, the
stack going into the upload box, the rest of the sheet rushing past, and out
of the count.

**Mix:** −14.5 LUFS integrated, a little under the other films; true peak
below −1 dBTP on the delivered AAC (the limiter's ceiling is −3 dB for this
film, as the track is mastered hot).

## 6. Delivery

Rendered at twice the size: each format at its usual size (scaled down from
the large frames) and as `-4k`; H.264 High, 60 fps, AAC 256k.
