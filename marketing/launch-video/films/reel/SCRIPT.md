# AI that works: the reel

A 27-second reel for n.abl's AI services, cut to music with no voice, in
every social format from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16. It shows
three services: a chat and voice booking agent, a sales co-pilot that
joins calls, and a document analyser. It is made in the style of a
reference reel the brief supplied: a product reel for the Opal app by
mc-visuals. Section 1 analyses that reel; section 2 is how this one uses
it.

Everything in it (the music, the sound effects and every frame) is
generated by the scripts in this folder. See [`README.md`](../../README.md)
to rebuild it.

---

## 1. The reference

The file is 16 s: about 10.2 s of film, a 1.5 s credit (the maker's
address, fading out), then TikTok's own 3.5 s download outro, which is not
part of the edit. 16:9, 30 fps.

### Music

- **Tempo and feel.** About 132.5 BPM, four-on-the-floor electronic. A long
  pitched "boom" kick on every beat: in the spectrogram each kick is a
  sweep from about 1 kHz down to 45 Hz. Claps on two and four, open hats
  on the offbeats, closed sixteenths around them, sustained chords, a
  tonal centre around D.
- **Shape.** The first second is a filtered build: bright hats and a
  rising sweep, no kick. The kick drops at 1.2 s. After that the track
  runs at one steady level (within about 2 dB) to the logo, carries on
  under the logo, and fades over the credit.

### Sound effects and sync

- **The drop is a camera move.** The kick arrives exactly as the camera
  finishes tilting the chart up to face the viewer.
- **Every transition lands on a kick,** within a frame or two:
  | Time | Move |
  |---|---|
  | 2.1 s | flare to a point |
  | 3.3 s | second pill |
  | 4.2 s | score tree |
  | 5.2 s | cut to the app icon |
  | 6.2 s | pixel dissolve |
  | 6.65 s | padlock |
  | 7.25 s | pull-out |
  | 8.35 s | Start button |
  | 8.9 s | the press |
  | 9.0 s | logo |
- **The effects are sparse and sit inside the music:**
  - UI blips as pills pop and numbers count;
  - a burst of noise and digital glitch under the pixel dissolve;
  - a click as the padlock closes;
  - a low boom under the press into the logo.
- **The music plays the story beat.** While the red padlock holds (6.8 to
  7.3 s), the whole track is low-passed: everything above about 2 kHz goes.
  It opens again on the pull-out.

### Editing: one camera, no cuts

There are almost no cuts. It plays as one continuous virtual camera, and
each element becomes the next through light:
1. A bar fills cyan, then orange, flares white and collapses to a point.
2. The point opens into a pill whose number counts up while a ring draws
   around it.
3. Two more pills join. A bracket gathers the three into a score.
4. A grey wash brings on an app icon.
5. A falling point of light hits the icon, which pixel-dissolves into a
   padlock and glows red.
6. The camera pulls out to the settings screen.
7. Rack focus to a Start button. The press. The logo blurs in.

A new idea every second or so (two beats).

### Camera

- A low glide over a chart lying almost flat (about 70° of tilt), pushing
  slowly forward.
- A fast tilt up to face-on, smeared vertically, landing on the drop.
- Slow drifts and push-ins while things count.
- A drift upward as the score builds, so the pills sink out of frame.
- A quick pull-out with vertical motion blur to reveal the next layer.
- Rack focus between planes.

### Blur and masking

- **Depth of field** on the perspective shot: soft near and far, a sharp
  band through the middle.
- **Blur-in:** every element and label enters through a Gaussian blur,
  soft and slightly large to sharp in 0.2 to 0.3 s, and leaves the same
  way.
- **Motion blur** on every fast camera move.
- **A red and cyan colour split** on the icon's entrance.
- **A blocky pixel dissolve** (magenta and cyan squares) as the mask for
  the icon-to-padlock morph.
- **Bloom** in cyan, orange and red: the only colour in the film.

### Look

- Near-black stage lit softly from above: a grey haze at the top, one grey
  wash as a transition.
- White interface with thin glowing rims and rounded pills.
- A clean, SF-style sans, small.
- Colour only where it means something.

## 2. This reel

Same grammar, n.abl's palette:
- A warm near-black stage lit from above.
- Cream interface.
- Colour only for meaning: ice blue for listening, amber for n.abl, coral
  for a warning.

The site's own typefaces: Inter Tight for interface, JetBrains Mono for
labels. There is no voice, so each service gets a chapter title for its
first three seconds, then gets out of the way. Each step is held long
enough to read: the answer, the translation, the objection, the suggestion,
the tags and the score each stay up for a second or more.

**The camera** is a real 3D camera, not a flat pan:
- It swings, orbits, tilts, pans and dollies on a smooth path through
  keyframes, following what happens.
- It smears the frame along its own motion, and only when it moves fast.
- Specks of light hang at every depth, so every move reads as space.
- No shot holds still.

## 3. Sound

**Music** (`audio.py`, style `reel`): house at 124 BPM in D minor.
- **The groove:**
  - a punchy house kick on every beat;
  - claps on two and four in a small room;
  - an open hat on every offbeat, shakers on the sixteenths between;
  - a ride in the last section.
- **The bass** rolls on the offbeat eighths, with sixteenth pick-ups once
  the co-pilot is on.
- **The harmony:**
  - saw chord stabs, detuned, with a filter that snaps shut, on the
    syncopated sixteenths between the kicks;
  - a supersaw pad under the last section;
  - everything harmonic pumped by the kick.
- **The hook** is a plucked lead (saw, pulse and a soft sine) with a
  dotted-eighth echo. It is doubled an octave up for the documents.
- **The cowbell** is left only as a faint nod to the reference, once every
  two bars.
- **Chords,** a bar each from the drop: Dm, B♭, F, C, then Dm, B♭, C and A
  to turn round.
- **It builds with the film:**
  - before the drop: the chord as a pad and the hook, behind a closed
    filter;
  - the receptionist: groove, bass and stabs;
  - the co-pilot: the lead joins; everything goes muffled while the
    objection glows;
  - the documents: everything;
  - a snare run and a riser into each chapter;
  - silence for the press;
  - the groove once more under the name, thinning out to the end.

**Effects** sit under the music and are drowned. Every one goes through
the same chain (`SFX_DROWN` in `film.py`):
- a steep low-pass at 1.4 kHz;
- a slow waver in pitch, as under water (a delay line swept a few
  milliseconds);
- a short, dark room.

The drops are an octave lower than in the last cut, eased in with no click.
The effects follow what each moment is, rather than a drop for everything:
- **Chat:** a soft two-note arrival for the voice note, muffled typing as
  it is transcribed, bubbles under the typing dots, the arrival again for
  the answer, a lifting swish for "Yes please", and a warm chime for
  Booked. A deep bubble as the booking flares into a point.
- **Booking:** soft bubbles as each pill lands, muffled ticks as the
  deposit counts, a small chime for the reminder.
- **Call:** a two-tone connect as the call opens, a falling tone and a
  bubble as the co-pilot drops in, muffled typing for the transcript, a
  glitch under the pixel dissolve, a shimmer as the English arrives.
- **Objection:** a low swell with a flutter while the music goes muffled
  (no alarm).
- **Sidebar:** soft slides for each block, typing for the suggestion, a
  small chime for the pivot.
- **Documents:** paper for the riffle, a low hum riding down the page with
  the scan, bubbles as things are marked, ticks as the pills count, the
  bracket drawn, and a three-note chime for the score.
- **Button and name:** a click for the press, an impact and a soft
  shimmer for the name.
- Whooshes for the camera's big moves, soft.

**Mix.** The effects sit about 5 dB under the music, with their energy
centred around 440 Hz. Master at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak below
−1 dBTP on the delivered AAC.
