# AI that works: the reel

A 17-second reel for n.abl's AI services, cut to music with no voice, in
every social format from one timeline: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16. It shows
three services: a chat and voice booking agent, a sales co-pilot that
joins calls, and a document analyser. It is made in the style of a
reference reel the brief supplied: a product reel for the Opal app by
mc-visuals. Section 1 analyses that reel; section 2 is how this one uses
it.

Everything in it (the music, the sound effects and every frame) is
generated by the scripts in this folder. See [`README.md`](../../README.md)
to rebuild it.

---

## 1. The reference

The file is 16 s: about 10.2 s of film, a 1.5 s credit (the maker's
address, fading out), then TikTok's own 3.5 s download outro, which is not
part of the edit. 16:9, 30 fps.

### Music

- **Tempo and feel.** About 132.5 BPM, four-on-the-floor electronic. A long
  pitched "boom" kick on every beat: in the spectrogram each kick is a
  sweep from about 1 kHz down to 45 Hz. Claps on two and four, open hats
  on the offbeats, closed sixteenths around them, sustained chords, a
  tonal centre around D.
- **Shape.** The first second is a filtered build: bright hats and a
  rising sweep, no kick. The kick drops at 1.2 s. After that the track
  runs at one steady level (within about 2 dB) to the logo, carries on
  under the logo, and fades over the credit.

### Sound effects and sync

- **The drop is a camera move.** The kick arrives exactly as the camera
  finishes tilting the chart up to face the viewer.
- **Every transition lands on a kick,** within a frame or two:
  | Time | Move |
  |---|---|
  | 2.1 s | flare to a point |
  | 3.3 s | second pill |
  | 4.2 s | score tree |
  | 5.2 s | cut to the app icon |
  | 6.2 s | pixel dissolve |
  | 6.65 s | padlock |
  | 7.25 s | pull-out |
  | 8.35 s | Start button |
  | 8.9 s | the press |
  | 9.0 s | logo |
- **The effects are sparse and sit inside the music:**
  - UI blips as pills pop and numbers count;
  - a burst of noise and digital glitch under the pixel dissolve;
  - a click as the padlock closes;
  - a low boom under the press into the logo.
- **The music plays the story beat.** While the red padlock holds (6.8 to
  7.3 s), the whole track is low-passed: everything above about 2 kHz goes.
  It opens again on the pull-out.

### Editing: one camera, no cuts

There are almost no cuts. It plays as one continuous virtual camera, and
each element becomes the next through light:
1. A bar fills cyan, then orange, flares white and collapses to a point.
2. The point opens into a pill whose number counts up while a ring draws
   around it.
3. Two more pills join. A bracket gathers the three into a score.
4. A grey wash brings on an app icon.
5. A falling point of light hits the icon, which pixel-dissolves into a
   padlock and glows red.
6. The camera pulls out to the settings screen.
7. Rack focus to a Start button. The press. The logo blurs in.

A new idea every second or so (two beats).

### Camera

- A low glide over a chart lying almost flat (about 70° of tilt), pushing
  slowly forward.
- A fast tilt up to face-on, smeared vertically, landing on the drop.
- Slow drifts and push-ins while things count.
- A drift upward as the score builds, so the pills sink out of frame.
- A quick pull-out with vertical motion blur to reveal the next layer.
- Rack focus between planes.

### Blur and masking

- **Depth of field** on the perspective shot: soft near and far, a sharp
  band through the middle.
- **Blur-in:** every element and label enters through a Gaussian blur,
  soft and slightly large to sharp in 0.2 to 0.3 s, and leaves the same
  way.
- **Motion blur** on every fast camera move.
- **A red and cyan colour split** on the icon's entrance.
- **A blocky pixel dissolve** (magenta and cyan squares) as the mask for
  the icon-to-padlock morph.
- **Bloom** in cyan, orange and red: the only colour in the film.

### Look

- Near-black stage lit softly from above: a grey haze at the top, one grey
  wash as a transition.
- White interface with thin glowing rims and rounded pills.
- A clean, SF-style sans, small.
- Colour only where it means something.

## 2. This reel

Same grammar, n.abl's palette:
- A warm near-black stage lit from above.
- Cream interface.
- Colour only for meaning: ice blue for listening, amber for n.abl, coral
  for a warning.

The site's own typefaces: Inter Tight for interface, JetBrains Mono for
labels. There is no voice, so each service is named once, in a small
chapter label (top left, or top centre in the tall formats).

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | **Chat** | A conversation lies flat: two chat bubbles and a live call whose voice streams through as bars. Soft near and far, the camera gliding over it. The build, then the tilt up to face-on, smeared, landing on the drop (1.36 s). The voice as seven levels: the one now rises ice blue, turns amber as it crosses the line, while *"Can I book in for Friday at 2?"* blurs in word by word. It flares white and collapses to a point. |
| 2.27 | **Book** | The point opens into a pill; its slot rolls Mon, Tue, Wed… to *Fri 2:00 pm*, a rim draws round it: *Booked*. On the next beats: *£20 paid* (Deposit) counting up, *Thu 6 pm* (Reminder sent). The camera drifts up into a grey wash. |
| 4.55 | **Call** | A video call drops in out of the grey, its colours split. A point of light falls from above and becomes *n.abl co-pilot joined*. The client's Spanish is transcribed word by word, then pixel-dissolves into English: *It's a bit pricey for us…* (ES → EN). *Objection · price*: the call glows red, its rim turns coral, and the music goes muffled. |
| 7.27 | **Coach** | Pull out with a vertical smear to the co-pilot's sidebar beside the call: the transcript, the playbook card (*Pricing objection: reframe on value, offer a pilot*), *Say next*, typed in (*"Most teams start with a 30-day pilot, so you only pay once it's working."*), and *Pivot → ROI*. |
| 8.64 | **Docs** | Rack focus into a riffle of documents (a contract, a staff handbook, a board pack, a CV) that settles on a tender pack. A line of light scans down it and marks a deadline, a risk and a requirement, each tagged beside the page. The page goes, and the tags fly down into three pills counting up: 42 requirements, 3 risks, 2 deadlines. A bracket draws them together into *Bid fit 86 ▲*. |
| 12.27 | **Button** | *Put AI to work* racks into focus, a light sweeps across it, and it is pressed on the beat. The music stops dead. |
| 13.18 | **Name** | The wordmark blurs into focus on the hit, under the light: *AI, built into your business.* Then *Book a free discovery call · nabl.agency*, and out. |

The document example is a tender pack, not an invoice. The riffle before
it says "any document".

All names, figures and scores are props in an interface, not claims:
*Client · Madrid*, *£20*, *42 / 3 / 2*, *Bid fit 86*.

## 3. Sound

- **Music** (`audio.py`, style `reel`), 132 BPM in D minor:
  - a long pitched boom kick on every beat;
  - claps on two and four, open hats on the offbeats, closed sixteenths;
  - a rolling sub bass between the kicks;
  - house stabs on the offbeats;
  - a plucked hook an octave up, with an echo;
  - a soft pad pumped by the kick.

  Chords Dm9, B♭maj7, Fmaj7 and C6, a bar each. It opens on Dm9 behind a
  closed filter and a riser, and drops on the tilt (1.36 s). It is
  muffled through a low-pass while the objection glows. It stops dead for
  the press. It comes back on the name with a crash and runs two bars
  under it, thinning out to the end.
- **Effects,** cued by the animation itself:
  - the riser, the whoosh of the tilt, a shimmer as the voice is
    understood, and the implode into the point;
  - a pop, ticks for the rolling slot, and blips as numbers land;
  - the fall and pop of the co-pilot joining;
  - soft keys as words are typed;
  - a glitch under the pixel dissolve;
  - an alert and a hit on the objection;
  - whooshes for the pull-outs and rack focus;
  - paper for the riffle, the scan, and a draw for the bracket;
  - the click of the button;
  - an impact and shimmer on the name.
- **Mix.** Master at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak below −1 dBTP on the
  delivered AAC.
