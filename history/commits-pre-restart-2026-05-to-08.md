### 2026-08-15 08:41 · `af33347` · Security: strengthen the portal credential, add rate limiting, harden delivery

Author: Claude

The portal access key is the only thing standing between the internet and a
client's quotes, projects, meetings and documents. It was weak in two ways
and unthrottled in a third.

CREDENTIAL
- Entropy was 20.7 bits: four characters over a 36-glyph alphabet, behind a
  prefix derived from the company name and a hardcoded year. ~1.7M candidates.
- Worse, it came from Math.random(). V8's xorshift128+ state is recoverable
  from a handful of outputs, so seeing a few issued keys could let you predict
  the next ones. That is not a credential generator.
- Now 12 characters from crypto.getRandomValues over a 31-glyph ambiguity-free
  alphabet (no O/0/I/1/L): ~59.5 bits, with rejection sampling so there is no
  modulo bias. Brute force is out of reach even with no throttling at all.
- Existing keys keep working and should be rotated; the format change only
  affects newly issued ones.

RATE LIMITING (new portal_login RPC, applied live)
Measured first: 30 wrong keys in a row were all answered 200. No throttling
existed anywhere.

v1 counted failures per IP. Its own live test failed, for the right reason —
the twelve probe requests left over two egress addresses, six each, so neither
hit the limit. Per-IP counting is defeated by trivial IP rotation.

v2 therefore checks the key BEFORE throttling, so a correct key is always
honoured and an attacker cannot burn a client's allowance to lock them out,
and counts misses on three axes: per IP (10), per key-prefix (25) and global
(300) per 15 minutes. The prefix axis is the one IP rotation cannot dodge,
since guesses against one client all share it. Verified live: 30 rotating-IP
guesses blocked after 9, while the real key for that prefix still signed in.
Attempts are logged by SHA-256 fingerprint, never by key, and the log is
unreadable by anon.

DELIVERY
- Added a CSP. script-src stays 'self' with no unsafe-inline, which is the
  clause that actually constrains XSS; connect-src is allow-listed to Supabase
  and Web3Forms; object-src and frame-ancestors are 'none'.
- Added HSTS, Permissions-Policy, COOP, and tightened X-Frame-Options to DENY.

PRIVACY
- Fonts were loaded from Google, disclosing every visitor's IP to a third
  party on page load, and the policy did not mention it. Now self-hosted
  (214 KB, latin + latin-ext variable faces), verified with zero requests to
  google/gstatic. This also let the CSP drop both third-party origins.
- The policy's 'no analytics or tracking' claim was checked and is accurate.
- Set the last-updated date, which was still a placeholder.

ALSO
- Fixed the mutable search_path on set_updated_at flagged by Supabase's
  advisor.
- Deleted the stale nabl website/ copies of the old static pages.
- npm run test:security encodes all of the above so it cannot silently rot;
  LIVE=1 also probes the real throttle.

### 2026-08-16 23:50 · `4cc8687` · Measure conversion without breaking the privacy promise

collected is personal data, so there is nothing to disclose, retain,
export or erase.
