How to record

With your webcam here: hit Use my webcam, then Clean view, then screen-record the stage. Everything is already composited.

Keying it in your editor:

  1. Leave it on Key colour and turn Guides off.
  2. Clean view, then screen-record the 9:16 stage.
  3. Drop that recording over your webcam take in CapCut, Premiere or OBS.
  4. Chroma-key the green #00B140 out. Your face lands in the window and moves with it.
  5. Two sections put type over a full-frame camera. Keying those would fringe the letters green, so record a second pass with Text off and lay the type back over the top — or just use the webcam mode, which has no key to fringe.
